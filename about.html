About Alena Supermart

A neighbourhood shop built on fresh food and fair prices.

Our story

Alena Supermart opened in Kutus with a simple goal: give local families a place to buy good food at prices that make sense. No inflated shelf prices, no wilted vegetables at the back of the crate, no queues that eat your lunch break.

We buy produce directly from growers around Kirinyaga every morning. That means shorter supply chains, fresher stock, and prices that compete with the open market rather than the big chains in town.

Today we serve thousands of households across Kutus, Kagio and the surrounding villages — in store, by phone, and through same-day delivery.

How a delivery works

  1. Send your list. Call, WhatsApp, or use the form on the contact page. No app or account needed.
  2. We confirm. We check stock and send you the total with any substitutions noted.
  3. We pack. Fresh items are packed last, so they reach you in the best condition.
  4. We deliver. Same afternoon anywhere in Kutus. Free on orders over KES 1,500.
